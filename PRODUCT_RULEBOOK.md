# TouchLine Product Rulebook — Canonical Entry Point

Status: CURRENT AUTHORITATIVE INDEX
Last reviewed: 2026-08-13

Product authority is organized in [TouchLine HQ](touchline-hq/README.md), especially `02_Product`, `03_Fantasy`, `04_Economy`, `05_Transfer_Center`, and `09_UI_UX`.

Permanent current card/data rules:

- TouchLine UUID is internal player identity; names are never identity keys.
- External football facts stay separate from TouchLine game state.
- Manual Admin owns approved Market Value; Card Engine owns tier, visual identity, and nominal card price.
- Public cards require canonical publication state.
- Do not invent football facts, values, tiers, prices, results, or commercial eligibility.
- Existing contract, wallet, scoring, and payment rules may change only in a separately authorized mission.

Use `CURRENT_STATE.md` for the current release state. Dated audit reports preserve evidence and do not override this rulebook unless explicitly marked current.
