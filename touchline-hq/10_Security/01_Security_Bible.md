## Objective

The Security Bible defines how Touchline protects users, identities, documents, credits, negotiations and marketplace integrity.

## Core Security Principles

1. Protect user identity.
2. Protect private football business documents.
3. Protect Touchline Credits.
4. Protect negotiations inside Touchline.
5. Prevent fraud and off-platform exploitation.
6. Keep permissions explicit.
7. Log important business actions.
8. Never expose secrets to the frontend.

## Secure Transfer Policy

All Touchline Card negotiations must stay inside Touchline.
