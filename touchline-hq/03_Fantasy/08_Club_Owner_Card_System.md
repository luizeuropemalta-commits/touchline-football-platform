## Executive Summary

The Club Owner Card is the most prestigious card in Touchline.

It uses the same premium design language as Touchline Player Cards, but the information displayed changes from player performance to club ownership identity, financial power, reputation, ranking and legacy.

The Club Owner Card is not just a profile card. It is a status symbol.

Its purpose is to make the user feel:

```text
I am building a football empire.
```

# 01 — Automatic AI Avatar

## Objective

Create a premium football business identity for every Club Owner.

## Workflow

The owner uploads one normal photo.

Touchline automatically generates a premium business portrait.

## Avatar Requirements

The avatar must:

- Preserve facial identity.
- Wear an elegant business suit.
- Look like a professional football club owner.
- Keep the same visual identity as Touchline Player Cards.
- Feel premium, serious and football-business oriented.
- Avoid cartoonish, childish or exaggerated styling.

## Visual Direction

The avatar should feel like:

- Club boardroom.
- Stadium executive suite.
- Premium football presentation.
- Modern console-game quality.
- Professional business portrait.

## Risks

- Avatar may distort facial identity.
- Avatar may look too artificial.
- Avatar may feel less professional if over-stylized.
- Users may upload inappropriate photos.

## Future Expansion

- Seasonal owner portraits.
- Trophy celebration portrait.
- Transfer deadline portrait.
- Club colors integration.
- Animated owner card reveal.

# 03 — Club Net Worth

## Objective

Club Net Worth is the official power metric for the Club Owner Card.

## Formula

Club Net Worth is calculated automatically:

```text
Club Net Worth =
Cash
*
Official Market Value of all 35 registered players
*
Club Assets
*
Financial Obligations
```

## Interpretation

This formula is a strategic economic score, not a real-world company valuation.

It combines:

- Cash strength.
- Squad value.
- Club assets.
- Financial obligations.

## Important Rule

Official player market values must always come from the selected football data provider.

Touchline does not manually change official market values to inflate Club Net Worth.

## Risks

- Multiplication can create extreme numbers if not normalized.
- Users may misunderstand Net Worth as real money.
- Financial obligations must reduce, not increase, strength.

## Recommended Improvement

Before implementation, define normalized scoring multipliers:

```text
Club Net Worth Score =
Cash Score
*
Squad Value Score
*
Asset Multiplier
*
Obligation Modifier
```

This prevents unrealistic numbers while preserving the intended logic.

# 04 — Prestige Levels

## Objective

Prestige Levels give Club Owners a visible status ladder.

## Rule

Owner Card levels are based only on Club Net Worth.

## Levels

- Bronze.
- Silver.
- Gold.
- Emerald.
- Diamond.

## Design Principle

Prestige must feel earned through long-term club building.

It must not be based directly on user spending.

## Risks

- If Net Worth is too tied to purchased credits, prestige becomes pay-to-win.
- If the gap between levels is too high, new users may feel excluded.
- If prestige changes too often, identity feels unstable.

## Future Expansion

- Seasonal prestige.
- All-time prestige.
- Regional prestige.
- Division prestige.
- Founder prestige.

# 06 — Automatic Updates

## Objective

The Club Owner Card must feel alive and connected to the ecosystem.

## Auto-Update Triggers

The Owner Card updates automatically whenever:

- Ranking changes.
- Credits change.
- Net Worth changes.
- Titles change.
- Squad Market Value changes.

## Data Principles

- Updates must be traceable.
- Users should understand why their card changed.
- Major changes should show a small history note.

## Risks

- Incorrect provider data can damage trust.
