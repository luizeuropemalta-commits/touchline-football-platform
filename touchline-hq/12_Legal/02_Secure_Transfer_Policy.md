## Legal Position

Touchline Card negotiations must happen exclusively inside Touchline.

Touchline may block messages, reduce Reputation Score, suspend users or review accounts when users attempt to move Touchline Card negotiations outside the ecosystem.

## Prohibited Conduct

Users may not share:

- Phone numbers.
- Email addresses.
- WhatsApp numbers.
- Telegram handles.
- Discord usernames or invites.
- Instagram usernames.
- Facebook links.
- X/Twitter links.
- PIX keys.
- IBAN numbers.
- PayPal information.
- Crypto wallet addresses.
- QR codes.
- External payment requests.

## Legal Purpose

This protects:

- Users from fraud.
- Marketplace integrity.
- Dispute resolution.
- Touchline Credits.
- Platform revenue.
- Compliance records.

## Terms of Service Requirement

The Terms of Service should include a clause stating that Touchline may monitor, block and moderate negotiation messages to prevent fraud, external payment solicitation and marketplace bypassing.
