## Objective

The Legal Bible defines the legal foundations that protect Touchline, its users and its ecosystem.

## Core Legal Principles

1. Touchline Cards do not represent real-world football ownership.
2. Touchline Credits are internal platform credits.
3. Touchline Fantasy must not be designed around gambling.
4. External data must be used legally and with source discipline.
5. Touchline negotiations must remain inside the platform.
