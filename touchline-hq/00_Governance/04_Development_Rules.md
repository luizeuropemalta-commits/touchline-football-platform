## Rules

1. Do not change production code during documentation-only missions.
2. Preserve existing work unless a change is explicitly approved.
3. Avoid rebuilding from scratch when a safe expansion is possible.
4. Fix root causes, not only visual symptoms.
5. Test important workflows before reporting completion.
6. Separate demo data from real data.
7. Do not leave dead buttons in production experiences.
8. Do not expose secret keys in client code.
9. Record major product and architecture decisions in Touchline HQ.
10. Push only reviewed, intentional changes.
