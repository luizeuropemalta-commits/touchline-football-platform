## Principles

1. Build for football professionals, not for generic users.
2. Make the platform useful before making it addictive.
3. Make the platform trustworthy before making it viral.
4. Protect user data, documents and identity.
5. Treat agents, clubs and players as serious professionals.
6. Avoid fake buttons, fake data and fake workflows.
7. Prefer fewer strong features over many weak features.
8. Build systems that can scale globally.
9. Challenge every assumption before implementation.
10. Preserve strategic memory through documentation.
