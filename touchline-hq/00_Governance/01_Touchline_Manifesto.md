# Touchline Manifesto

Version: 0.1  
Author: Touchline Executive Documentation Board  
Last Updated: 2026-06-26  
Status: Active  
Dependencies: 01_Executive, 02_Product  
Future Related Documents: Touchline Vision, Company Mission, Founder Principles

Touchline exists to become the global football business operating system.

Touchline must serve real football professionals first: agents, clubs, players, scouts, academies, coaches, investors and trusted football operators.

Touchline must not become a generic CRM.

Touchline must combine trust, data, identity, opportunity, negotiation, reputation and football emotion into one ecosystem.

## Constitutional Rules

1. Trust is more important than growth.
2. Verified relationships are more valuable than raw data.
3. External data must be treated as reference, not ownership.
4. The product must feel premium, fast and alive.
5. Every feature must create business value or emotional retention.
6. Touchline must protect professional credibility.
7. Touchline Fantasy and Touchline Professional must be connected but clearly separated.
8. Legal safety must never be sacrificed for speed.
9. No strategic idea should be deleted; obsolete ideas belong in `99_Archive`.
