## Purpose

Stores deprecated ideas, old documents and historical decisions.

Strategic ideas should never be deleted. They should be archived with context.

## Archive Rules

1. Do not delete strategic documents.
2. Move obsolete documents here.
3. Add a short note explaining why the document was archived.
4. Preserve original dates when possible.
5. Keep archive searchable.
