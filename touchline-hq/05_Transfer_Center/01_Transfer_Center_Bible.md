## Objective

The Transfer Center is the official place where Touchline Card negotiations, offers, counteroffers, loans, exchanges and clauses happen.

## Constitutional Rules

1. All negotiations stay inside Touchline.
2. Every proposal must be recorded.
3. Every counteroffer must be recorded.
4. Every acceptance or rejection must be recorded.
5. External payment and contact details are prohibited.
6. Secure Negotiation Rooms are mandatory for serious deals.
