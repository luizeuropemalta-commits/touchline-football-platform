# TouchLine stadium image attribution

## TouchLine aerial stadium collection — QA visual only

The twenty files under `aerial/` are optimized derivatives of the original
2048×2048 AI-generated TouchLine stadium-avatar drafts supplied by the product
owner for QA visual validation. They are used only inside the small Stadium
card in the Live Match Centre and are not the main match background.

Production use remains outside this mission and requires the separate visual
approval described by the source asset manifest.
